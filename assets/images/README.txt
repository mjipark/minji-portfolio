Drop your project images in this folder.

To use one, open the relevant page in projects/ and replace the placeholder
<div class="image-slot"><p class="placeholder-text">...</p></div>
with:
<div class="image-slot"><img src="../assets/images/your-file.jpg" alt="describe the image"></div>

Recommended: keep hero images landscape (roughly 16:10) so they crop cleanly.
